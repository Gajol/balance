## About
This site is a collection of things that interest me, both professionally and personally.  I have had a long career in technology, starting with telecommunications and then moving into enterprise information technology.

Leadership development, and how it influences technology's role in enabling the business, is a steady focus of my reading.  I am also interested in modern approaches to development using psychology and design thinking, in economic models and theories (including behavioral economics) and how they shape world policy and investment decisions, and in foreign policy and contemporary history.  Away from work, I ride a bike.
