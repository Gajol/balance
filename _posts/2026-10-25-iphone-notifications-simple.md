When someone sends you a message, your iPhone shows a small box on the screen. That small box is called a **notification**.

This page shows you how to read your notifications. Take your time. Every step has a picture.

## The one thing to remember

You can slide your finger up on the phone. But **where you start** changes what happens.

- Start in the **middle**. You see your messages.
- Start at the **very bottom edge**. The phone opens and your messages go out of sight.

![The iPhone lock screen. Number 1 points to the middle of the screen. Number 2 points to the thin white bar at the very bottom.]({{ '/assets/images/iphone-notifications/where-to-start.svg' | relative_url }})

The thin white bar at the very bottom is the one to be careful with.

## Way 1: Slide up from the middle

This is the best way. You see all your messages at once.

1. Wake the phone. Lift it, or press the side button once.
2. Look at the phone for a moment.
3. Put one finger in the **empty middle** of the screen.
4. Slide up slowly.
5. Your list of messages opens.
6. Tap a message to read it.

![Left: a finger slides up from the middle of the lock screen. Right: the list of messages opens.]({{ '/assets/images/iphone-notifications/slide-up-middle.svg' | relative_url }})

To put the list away, press the side button once. The screen goes dark.

## Way 2: Tap the pile

Messages from one person sit in a pile, like a stack of cards. You see only the top one.

1. Wake the phone.
2. Tap the pile **once**.
3. The pile opens. You see every message.

![Left: a finger taps the pile of messages. Right: the pile opens and each message shows.]({{ '/assets/images/iphone-notifications/tap-the-pile.svg' | relative_url }})

## Way 3: The phone is already open

Sometimes the phone is already open and you are looking at your apps. You can still see your messages.

1. Put one finger at the **top of the screen, in the middle**.
2. Pull down slowly.
3. Your list of messages opens.

![Left: the phone is open and a finger pulls down from the top middle. Right: the list of messages opens.]({{ '/assets/images/iphone-notifications/pull-down-top.svg' | relative_url }})

Start in the **middle** of the top. If you start in the top **right** corner, you open a different screen with switches and settings. Do not worry if that happens. Slide it back up and try again.

## The slide to avoid: from the very bottom

This slide opens the phone. It is useful when you want to use your apps. It is not the one to use when you only want to read messages.

![Left: a finger slides up from the thin white bar at the very bottom. Right: the phone opens to the Home Screen with apps.]({{ '/assets/images/iphone-notifications/slide-up-bottom.svg' | relative_url }})

If this happens, do not worry. Your messages are not lost. Press the side button once to lock the phone. Wake it again. Then try Way 1.

## If it does not work

- Start in the **middle**, not at the bottom edge.
- Use **one finger**. Slide **slowly**.
- Do you see only the word "Notification" and no words from the person? Your phone may hide the words until it knows it is you. Look straight at the phone for a second. Try again.
- Does your iPhone have a round button at the bottom? The pictures will look a little different. Sliding up from the middle works the same way.

## Quick reminder

| Where you start | What happens |
|---|---|
| Middle of the screen, slide up | You see your messages |
| Tap the pile | The pile opens |
| Phone open, top middle, pull down | You see your messages |
| Very bottom edge, slide up | The phone opens |

*The pictures are drawings. Your phone may look a little different, depending on the iPhone and the iOS version.*
