The plan for a first bilingual intranet release, in one read: the roadmap, a storyboard of who gains and when, eight screen concepts, ideas for later, and what personalization could mean.

Roadmap

Four phases and nine evidence-based gates over 58 weeks. After go-live, each wave moves one of four jobs. Waves are proposed, not committed.

Discover

Gates: requirements baseline (week 9); design sign-off (week 15). Workshops on content, IT and security.

Alpha

Gate: alpha accepted (week 30). Built on a connected network, core concepts only, no real data. Teams are trained here.

Isolated build

Gates: transfer pipeline dry run (34); integration (42); test and assurance (46); authority to operate (52).

Go-live

Gates: go-live (56); hypercare done and project closed (58). The old site goes dark.

The critical path runs through discovery, design, alpha build, pipeline, install, test, authorization and go-live. Publishing and lifecycle work sits off the chain but gates the content load. At test and assurance, English and French are counted separately.

Proposed

Wave 1

Phase 1 increment: My content, trust signals in search, owner nudges, retire with confidence.

Later

Wave 2

Service catalogue, My Tasks, calendar availability.

Year 2

Wave 3

While you were away, training catalogue, related pages, role-based views.

Year 3

Wave 4

Guided onboarding by role; task success on the top tasks.

Potential

Wave 5

Expertise registry. Needs an owner and a freshness rule first.

Storyboard

1. Seven steps to a video today, two at go-live

Today: seven steps

  1. Click a link on the intranet
  2. Land in the corporate document system
  3. Sign in
  4. Save the file
  5. Find the file
  6. Open it in a desktop player
  7. Hope the codec is installed

Go-live: two steps

  1. Click the page
  2. It plays in the browser, with captions in your language

One managed copy, with an owner and a review date. Views are measured.

The video is the visible symptom. Underneath it, pages have no named owner, nobody knows what is read, and nothing flags a stale page.

2. “One-stop shop” is four jobs; Phase 1 attacks one

Find

Locate information fast, in my language.

Directly attacked

Know

Learn what changed without hunting.

Partly

Do

Start my task: leave, travel, IT ticket.

Links only

Connect

Respond, ask or contribute.

One channel

Why this order. Engagement features on stale content make the problem worse, because they advertise neglect. Fix the content first.

5. Say what staff lose or do not get before they find out

Not in this release

  • Nothing is pushed: no digests or notifications to staff
  • No transactions: pay, leave and IT requests are links
  • No targeted announcements, follow-a-topic or personalization
  • No mobile; no live streams on the site (they link out)

Ruled out, not deferred

  • Open discussion forums
  • Wiki-style co-editing
  • A separate engagement platform
Lost on day one: policy in search. Policy moves to its own platform, which the intranet search cannot see. Mitigate with a banner and stub pages, and ask now for a feed that search can index.

Eight screen concepts

Eight bilingual, clickable screens let Communications and business lines agree the publishing experience before anyone builds it. Each one shows real-looking content and the unhappy paths. Only the pattern carries into the build, not the prototype code.

What personalization might mean

Familiar controls show what personalization could offer. Every one is an explicit choice by the person using the site. This section is my own read, not a decision.

BookmarksSave pages to come back to.
Follow a topicChanges to a shared taxonomy term appear in my digest.
Pinned and recentQuick return to what I use. Kept for me, not tracked.
Recently visitedA short list I can see and clear myself.
ProfileName, branch and role come from the identity feed. Nothing typed twice.
SettingsDigest on or off, display choices. Explicit choices only.
NotificationsA digest, never content in the email.
Language and role viewInterface follows my language; home shows what my role needs (a later wave).

1 Show what is mine

Branch and role come from the identity feed. Nothing is stored.

2 Let me choose

Bookmarks, follow and settings. I set them and I can clear them.

3 Learn about me

Recommendations from behaviour. Not advised: analytics is anonymized and aggregate, and personalization must not turn into tracking.

Not in the first release. If it comes later, build personal parts of a page as placeholders from the start, so the cache keeps working for signed-in staff.
