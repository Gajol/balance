Reusable ways of building features on the intranet platform. A pattern says how to solve a problem we meet more than once.

Principle

Why: what must be true.

Written once for the whole system (P1 to P6).

Pattern (this page)

How: a repeatable shape.

Reused wherever the same problem appears.

Decision (ADR)

What we chose.

One choice, with alternatives and a date.

Constraint

What must hold.

Pass or fail. Set by the EA, not the vendor.

Website experience

What staff see and do on the intranet.

  • Search and findability Staff find what they need, in either language.
    PAT-001 PAT-002 PAT-003
  • People and live status Directory cards, availability and other live values.
    PAT-005 PAT-006
  • Messages and reminders Email reminders, digests and review queues.
    PAT-004 PAT-009
  • Accessible experience and plain language Words not colour, readable by everyone, in both languages.
    PAT-007 PAT-010 PAT-013

Content and governance

How content is owned, labelled, measured and kept current.

  • Accessibility assurance Checks at build, at publishing and on the live site, with proof over time.
    PAT-011 PAT-013
  • Bilingual parity English and French equal at every layer, and provable.
    PAT-007 PAT-011 PAT-013
  • Ownership and freshness Every item has an owner and a review date, and a person decides.
    PAT-002 PAT-003 PAT-004 PAT-009 PAT-011
  • Taxonomy and labels One shared vocabulary in the CMS, search and analytics.
    PAT-001 PAT-007
  • Measurement and analytics Numbers for executives, Communications and IT.
    PAT-011 PAT-013

Architecture and platform

How the system is built, connected and kept safe.

  • Integration with other systems How the intranet reads from systems it does not own.

  • Dependencies and delivery What is allowed in, and how it crosses the boundary.
    PAT-008 PAT-010 PAT-012
